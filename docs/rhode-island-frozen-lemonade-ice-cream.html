Rhode Island frozen lemonade & creameries

26 places for Rhode Island's summer sweets: Del's frozen lemonade, scoop shops and creameries, and Newport Creamery's Awful Awful. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Lemonade, cabinets and the Awful Awful

Frozen lemonade, slushy and sharp with lemon, comes from roadside stands and trucks all summer; Del's is the name most people know. Newport Creamery's Awful Awful is an ice milk shake, and a coffee cabinet (a coffee-milk shake) is the state's other summer order. Brand facts only, no logos. Many stands close for the winter, and seasonal isn't closed.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Warwick (3), South Kingstown (2), Coventry (2), Cumberland (2), Middletown (2), Smithfield (2), Tiverton (2), Providence (2).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Clam shacks and lemonade stands close for the winter, and bakeries can sell out of strips by noon, so check before you drive.
